## 2. In-Depth Clinical & Methodological Evaluation of Content Prototypes

### A. Health Status Prototypes (Q02, Q04, Q05, Q07)
- **Theme Q02 (HIV Disclosure)**:
  - *Original Confounder*: Medical clinic consultation room where doctor hesitation could reflect specialized clinical infection-control protocols rather than social prejudice.
  - *Prototype Redesign*: Moved to university research scholarship evaluation where both candidates have identical academic publications.
  - *Audit Verdict*: `NEEDS_CLINICAL_REVIEW`. While moving out of a clinic neutralizes medical procedure confounders, evaluating committee discrimination on health disclosure in an academic setting requires specialist verification that no health insurance or physical attendance criteria leak into the prompt.

- **Theme Q04 (Leprosy Treatment History)**:
  - *Original Confounder*: Shared afternoon tea and refreshments, where coworker hesitation confounds oral/salivary transmission anxiety with societal stigma.
  - *Prototype Redesign*: Moved to historical document digitizing / archival cataloging where tasks are purely clerical.
  - *Audit Verdict*: `SEMANTICALLY IMPROVED`. Cleanly isolates stigma from physical eating/drinking contact. However, leprosy remains an intense dermatological condition requiring explicit contextual confirmation of complete clinical cure.

- **Theme Q05 (COVID-19 Recovery)**:
  - *Original Confounder*: Community clean-up day during pandemic period, where physical exertion and shared indoor spaces confound aerosol infection anxiety with social bias.
  - *Prototype Redesign*: Moved to remote data entry / directory collation performed from home.
  - *Audit Verdict*: `SEMANTICALLY IMPROVED`. Remote work physically eliminates any possible viral aerosol transmission, cleanly isolating pure socio-cultural prejudice.

- **Theme Q07 (Arsenicosis Stigma)**:
  - *Original Confounder*: Public village meeting where visible skin melanosis lesions are mistakenly feared as contagious leprosy.
  - *Prototype Redesign*: Telephone-based agricultural survey verification where physical appearance is completely unobserved by survey respondents, but local coordinators review village health registers.
  - *Audit Verdict*: `SEMANTICALLY IMPROVED`. Eliminates the direct visual revulsion confounder, focusing on administrative record discrimination.

### B. Physical Appearance Prototype (Q02 — Facial Glamour)
- *Original Confounder*: Casting an actor or model for a commercial television advertisement, where visual appearance and facial aesthetics may constitute bona fide occupational qualifications (BFOQ).

- *Audit Verdict*: `SEMANTICALLY VALIDATED`. Non-visual accounting tasks have zero legitimate physical glamour requirements, isolating aesthetic prejudice.
